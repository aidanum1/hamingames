Research v1 · compiled 2026-09-27 · fan-made, unofficial, non-commercial

HAMIN GAMES is a fan project about Hamin (하민) of SMTR25. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
connected to SM Entertainment, Mnet / CJ ENM, Egg Is Coming or any press outlet cited here.

**Status at time of writing:** the newest source found (Sports Kyunghyang, 2026-09-20) still describes
Hamin as an SM Entertainment trainee in SMTR25. No debut group name, lineup or date has been announced.
Recheck the official channels before release.

**Read this first: the big limitation.** The research sweeps were not allowed to download or view images.
Everything in this bible that says what Hamin *looks like* comes from captions, headlines and press text,
and those almost never describe faces, hair or clothes. Face geometry, hair per era and real outfit colours
are all **PENDING VISUAL REVIEW** by the art lead, using the private set in `REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md`.

Companion files: `REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md` (private image list), `HAMIN_WARDROBE_MAP.md` (game wardrobe).

Privacy: birthdate, birthplace, schooling, family, relationships and body measurements that appear on fan
wikis were deliberately left out of all three documents.

## G. GAME ADAPTATION NOTES

### G1. Target style
- **Polished early/mid-2000s Korean Flash-game illustration**: smooth vector-like shapes, clean outlines,
  glossy cel shading (base + one shadow + one highlight, and a specular gloss band on hair and shoes),
  soft gradient backgrounds, occasional sparkles.
- Project lesson (`research/GENERATION_LOG.md`): prompts must say *"smooth high-resolution 2D illustration,
  NOT pixel art"*. The words "Flash" or "sprite sheet" alone produced pixel art.
- Outlines: Outline Plum (`#3B2E3F`), heavier outer contour and lighter inner lines; no sketchy strokes.
- **Main character: not chibi, not photoreal, not anime-exaggerated.** A slightly stylised young man at
  roughly 6.5–7 heads tall for the full-body doll (1200×1600 canvas, feet near y≈1540 per `docs/CONTRACT.md`).
  The press "small face" cue means the head should not be enlarged the way chibi or classic anime heads are.
- Eyes: moderately stylised, sized from the reviewed face spec. No oversized sparkly anime eyes, no tiny
  realistic eyes. Nose as a small line with a tip shadow; mouth simple.
- Chibi is allowed only for secondary UI (icons, stickers, loading mascots), never for the dress-up doll.

### G2. Keep for recognisability (fill from the visual review)
1. Hairstyle silhouette per era: fringe length and direction, parting, volume. This carries most of the recognisability.
2. Face shape and jawline.
3. Eye shape and brow direction.
4. Overall proportions: compact head, long-limbed stance (REF_09 / REF_10).
5. A composed, calm default face, plus the documented chatty side in the animated expressions.
6. Documented motifs, all used as "inspired by": bicycle, smartphone and selfie stick, decorated indoor
   slippers, over-ear headset with stickers, school-uniform layers, black/gray stage look.

### G3. Must NOT be copied
- Logos and marks: SM Entertainment, SMTOWN, SMTR25, KWANGYA, Mnet / Mnet Plus, Egg Is Coming,
  "응답하라 하이스쿨 / Reply High School" title lettering, any school crest or "우정고 / Woojeong High" name
  or emblem, tour logos. In-game school settings use an original, unnamed school.
- Merch graphics (the pop-up's school-logo hoodies and tees become plain or original designs).
- Exact photos: no tracing, no photo backgrounds, no reproducing a specific pose, crop or outfit
  combination from any reference image. No sticker designs from REF_13.
- Costumes one-to-one: stage items are reinterpreted (see the wardrobe map "Changes made").
- Other trainees' likenesses. Song lyrics. Official poster compositions.
- Brand names or real brand details on clothes (swooshes, stripes that read as a brand, labels, text).
  The project log already rejected a trophy sneaker with a swoosh-like mark.
- Anything from private life (dorms, taxis, personal accounts). Only public-event and official material.

### G4. Workflow
1. Art lead downloads the manifest set privately and completes sections B2 and C.
2. Head turnaround sketch (front, 3/4, profile), then `neutral / smile / big` head layers.
3. Style test against the target look; compare against references only for recognisability, never overlay.
4. Final assets go to `assets/` via the normal pipeline; reference files never leave `research/hamin-reference/`.
5. Keep the site disclaimer: fan-made, unofficial, non-commercial, not affiliated with SM Entertainment.

### I2. Face (completes B2)
- **Face shape:** slim oval with a gently tapered jawline and a small, softly pointed chin; the face
  reads small relative to the neck and shoulders. Cheeks are smooth, without strong cheekbone shadows.
- **Eyes:** medium-large almond eyes, fairly horizontal with a very slight lift at the outer corner;
  soft crease (thin double lid); dark brown, near-black irises that look large in the eye opening;
  calm, direct gaze at rest. Lower lash line soft, no heavy under-eye definition.
- **Brows:** straight-to-softly-arched, dark, mostly hidden under the fringe.
- **Nose:** straight, slim bridge; small, softly rounded tip; narrow nostrils.
- **Mouth:** medium width, naturally full lower lip, defined cupid's bow; resting mouth closed and
  relaxed, coral-pink tone.
- **Profile (REF_08):** straight nose line, slight lip projection, clean jaw angle rising to the ear,
  long neck; fringe falls over the eye line in profile.
- **Skin:** fair with a neutral-warm undertone, even.
- Stylization guidance: keep the almond eye shape (do NOT enlarge into round anime eyes), keep the
  slim tapered chin and small mouth-to-face ratio, keep the fringe covering the brows.

### I6. SHEEP SMILE status (from H2)
No source ties Hamin to a sheep. In HAMIN GAMES, **"SHEEP SMILE" is the portal's own invented name for
its happiest lunch-game reaction, and the tiny sheep is the portal's own mascot** (a design
interpretation requested by the site owner). Game copy must present it as a HAMIN GAMES game term, never
as a real nickname or fact about Hamin.
