> ## PRIVATE RESEARCH MATERIAL ONLY
> The images listed here are copyrighted by SM Entertainment, Mnet/CJ ENM and the press agencies
> credited below. They are **private study references** for drawing an original, stylized
> fan illustration of Hamin. They must **never** be copied into the public site, `assets/`,
> the deploy folder, a CDN, a repo that gets published, or any shared drive.
>
> - Planned storage: `research/hamin-reference/` (local only). Add this folder to every
>   deploy/ignore list before any file is saved there (not done yet; nothing has been downloaded).
> - Do not trace, paint over, crop into, or use these photos as backgrounds or as the base
>   image of an edit. Generated art must not reproduce a specific photo's pose, framing or outfit.
> - Uploading any of these files to a third-party generation service (for example an
>   `media_import_url` / `media_upload` step) is itself a copy to another server. The project
>   owner decides that per image; prefer official images and keep the uploaded set minimal.
> - Use only official and press-event images. Never add private-life, dorm, taxi or
>   paparazzi-style images (SM warned of legal action over trainee privacy violations in
>   March 2026: https://www.starnewskorea.com/star/2026/03/26/2026032600314192564).
